About

Useful tools, honestly described.

Tavren makes one thing: structured AI toolkits that help in-house SAP teams prepare for the move from ECC to S/4HANA. We'd rather show you than tell you — which is why the starter kit is free.

What we believe

An S/4HANA upgrade is one of the largest, most expensive programmes a business will run — and a great deal of that cost is work that an in-house team could do themselves, if they had the right structure to do it well. AI has made that genuinely possible. The missing piece is method: knowing what to ask, in what order, with what context.

That's what a Tavren toolkit is. Not a list of tips, and not a vague "prompt pack" — a professional playbook of structured prompts that produce repeatable, decision-ready outputs for a specific function.

How we keep it honest

  • No invented testimonials, no borrowed logos. We don't display social proof we haven't earned.
  • Try before you buy. The free starter kit is a real, professionally built toolkit, built to the same standard of craft as the packs — so you can see exactly the quality you're paying for.
  • Accurate claims about your data. You run these prompts in your own AI tool. Tavren never sees, stores, or receives your company data.
  • Plain pricing. Individual packs are $779; full series bundles are $3,099. No tiers, no upsells, no surprises.

Who it's for

Senior business people and process owners inside SAP customer organisations — in Finance, Sales, Supply Chain and HR — who want to take more of their upgrade in-house. If that's you, the toolkits are built for the work you already own.
