Cookie Policy

Last reviewed: 10 June 2026

We have deliberately built tavrensolutions.com to be light on tracking. This Site sets no first-party tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies for advertising or to profile visitors. Because of this, no cookie-consent banner is required to browse the Site.

What this means in practice

  • Analytics. We use Umami Analytics, which is cookieless by design — it sets no cookies and does not collect personal data.
  • Contact form. Our contact form (Web3Forms) does not set cookies.
  • No advertising or social-tracking cookies. We do not embed advertising pixels or social-media trackers.

Checkout (third-party)

When you choose to buy a toolkit, the secure checkout is provided by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. That checkout runs on Lemon Squeezy's own platform (opened in an overlay or a new tab) and may set its own cookies that are strictly necessary to operate the payment process. Those cookies are set by Lemon Squeezy on their domain and are governed by Lemon Squeezy's own cookie and privacy policies, not ours. They are only involved if and when you proceed to checkout.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Because we set no first-party tracking cookies, doing so will not affect your ability to browse this Site.
