Why Tavren

Four reasons in-house teams choose toolkits

Reduce consultancy cost

Implementation consultancy is typically the single largest line in an SAP upgrade budget — often hundreds of thousands, sometimes millions. Every readiness, data and testing task your team takes back in-house is work that never reaches that invoice.

Better upgrade readiness

Structured, repeatable preparation for the move from ECC to S/4HANA — so your team arrives at each stage knowing what to do.

Reduced project risk

Surface gaps, assumptions and data problems early — while there is still time to act on them, not after go-live.

SAP's AI tools aren't available yet

SAP's own AI — including Joule — is designed to run once you are live on the new system. It does not help your teams during the upgrade itself. The business readiness work needs to happen now, using the tools you already have. That is exactly what Tavren toolkits are built for.
